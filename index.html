Collections worth a scroll

These are organized by collection or artist, so the keyword box can't reach them.

  • → Mid-century Illustrated on Flickr. Member group, 1945 to 1965 posters, comics, recipes.
  • → leifpeng's collections on Flickr. Retro ads and cartoons sorted by studio and artist.
  • → Cartoon Modern on Internet Archive. The full book on 1950s animation design, searchable inside the reader.
  • → My Retro TV. 1950s and 60s commercials and cartoons, sorted by year.
  • → Spelunkr. Free 24/7 TV channels built from Internet Archive videos. Watch by channel, no keyword search.
  • → Heritage Type free vintage illustrations. A free page of downloads. Their site search returns mostly paid sets, so browse this page instead.
  • → Century Library. Paid membership with 50,000+ restored vintage assets. No free search, so check the license before you subscribe.
Before you use anything

Keep a source log

For every image you keep, note the URL, the publication date and the rights statement. It takes ten seconds now and saves a scramble later.

  • 1931 to 1963 US work may be public domain if its copyright was never renewed, but that has to be checked item by item.
  • CC0 on an aggregator doesn't prove the original is public domain.
  • Results are the first dozen per source, in each site's own relevance order. Use Show more or the See all link to go deeper.
